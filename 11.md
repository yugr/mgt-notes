1-1's are regular, private conversations between engineer and his manager about what's going on, what's ahead and what needs attention. They are primary for engineer to raise topics, ask questions, share concerns and get support.

What to expect: use 11 to share
  - what's going well (progress, achievements, learning, etc.)
  - raise anythiung that bothers you or blocks or may become a problem
  - spot "icebergs" i.e. potential long-term problems that are not visible enough yet
  - splot "eldorados" i.e. promising unexplored opportunities that can create value
  - ask for help
  - discuss growth (skills, responsibilities, feedback, career devevelopment)
  - share feedback (e.g. about project or processes)

What 1-1 is not:
  - status report
  - design discussion

How to prepare: think before the meeting
  - what should my manager know
  - what worries me
  - what should I warn about
  - what's worth exploring
  - should I request help
  - what questions (opinions) I never ask (share) on other meetings
